Every memecoin on Robinhood Chain that trades against a tokenized stock. To buy one you must first hold the equity, so a meme rally becomes real onchain demand for the share.

One row per stock. The bar is that ticker's memes, sized by liquidity and tinted by their move, biggest first. Click any segment to copy its contract address. Click the ticker for the full roster, the onchain price and how far it sits from the real one.

Each row is one tokenized stock and the three biggest memecoins trading against it, by market cap. Buying one of those memes means first holding the equity, so a meme rally becomes real onchain demand for the share. Hover a coin for its 24h shape, click to copy its contract, click the stock for the full roster. Quiet marks a coin with real liquidity that almost nobody traded in the last 24 hours. A crown means one coin holds both the deepest pool and the majority of the trading on that stock, so it has actually won the ticker rather than just parked liquidity in it. It needs at least three rivals to count, since being the only coin on a stock is not a win.
